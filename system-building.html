// faq

Desktop or web-based — which is better for me?

Desktop systems work offline and suit single-location businesses. Web-based systems are accessible from anywhere with internet. We can advise which fits your needs during scoping.

Can the system connect to our existing tools?

Yes — we can build integrations with other systems via APIs or direct database connections. Share what you use and we'll assess compatibility.

Can we add features after launch?

Yes. We build systems with future expansion in mind. Additions can be scoped and quoted as separate engagements after the initial build.
